What is KhataNova?

KhataNova is a simple digital khata and stock app for shops, godowns, wholesalers and small factories. Track item-wise stock, get low stock alerts, manage customer and supplier balances, and keep everything in one place with Excel-ready reports.

What does this app include?

  • Stock in/out with item-wise stock and low stock alerts
  • Customer and supplier ledger with WhatsApp statements
  • Bills and invoices: print, PDF and WhatsApp
  • Cash book and daily expenses
  • Staff attendance, salary and advances
  • Profit and sales reports with Download CSV
  • PIN lock, backup and restore
  • Any currency and country: tax/VAT/GST on invoices, kilogram or pound
  • Calculator, unit converter and cost and profit calculator

Frequently asked questions

How do I record stock in and out?

Open the Entry tab, choose Goods received (In) or Goods sent (Out), then enter the item, weight and party name and save. Stock updates automatically.

Does the app keep customer and supplier ledgers?

Yes. The Party tab shows each customer and supplier with bills, payments and the balance to receive or give.

Do I get a low stock alert?

Yes. Set a minimum stock for every item and the card turns red when stock falls below it.

Can I download my data?

Yes. Download the full record as a CSV file that opens in Excel, or take a JSON backup.

How do I create a bill or invoice?

Open Bills, tap Create new bill, enter the customer and goods and save. Stock is reduced and the ledger is updated. Print, save as PDF or share on WhatsApp.

How do I track staff attendance and salary?

Open Staff, add your staff and mark Present, Half day or Absent every day. Record salary and advances and the balance is calculated for you.

Where can I see my profit?

Open Reports to see profit, sales, purchases, expenses and top customers for today, 7 days, this month, this year or all time.

Does the app have a PIN lock?

Yes. Set a 4-digit PIN from App Lock on Home. The app locks again after 30 seconds away.

Can I use the app in my country and currency?

Yes. In Settings choose your currency symbol, number format, decimals, weight unit, WhatsApp country code and default tax, VAT or GST.

Does the app have a calculator?

Yes. Open Calculator for a basic calculator, a unit converter and a cost and profit calculator.
